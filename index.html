Our Story

Savor the True Taste of Lean Japanese Wagyu

The story begins in 1962. The first generation started this journey as a wagyu merchant.

We proudly serve ultra-rare lean wagyu, which accounts for only about 5% of the entire market. Carefully sourced directly from farmers, this premium beef is slow-cooked at low temperatures to unlock its deep umami and melt-in-your-mouth tenderness. No excessive fat — just the pure flavor of real wagyu.

Our signature juicy wagyu burgers are crafted with this exceptional lean beef, offering a unique local gourmet experience you won't find anywhere else. A must-try for international guests seeking the true taste of Japan.
